## Q1 — Two populations, one count

A library records 40 loans: 15 fiction and 25 nonfiction. Six fiction loans are overdue.

(a) What proportion of the fiction loans are overdue?
(b) What proportion of all loans are both fiction and overdue?
Name the population forming each denominator and explain why the denominators differ.

## Q2 — Follow the intermediate table

A 27-row table contains three groups with 7, 9, and 11 rows. Grouping alone is applied. Next, grouped mutate adds a computed column. Then grouped summarise returns one scalar summary for each of the original three groups.

How many rows are present after grouping, after mutate, and after summarise? Finally, a grouped mutate adds a computed column to the three-row summary table. How many rows does that final table contain? Explain each transition using the course rules.

## Q3 — Translate the condition

Each row below counts people, with each person in exactly one cell.

| Session | Remote | On-site |
|---|---:|---:|
| Morning | 12 | 8 |
| Evening | 6 | 14 |

“Among remote participants, what fraction attend the evening session?”

State the numerator population and denominator population, give their counts, and calculate the fraction. Explain whether the evening total or the all-participant total is the relevant denominator.
