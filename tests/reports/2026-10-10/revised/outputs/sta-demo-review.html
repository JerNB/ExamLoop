Three rules to retrieve

1. Name the denominator population

A group proportion uses that group’s total. A whole-table proportion uses the entire table total. Source: materials.md L1.

2. Follow the current table

Grouping alone keeps rows. Grouped mutate keeps its input rows and adds a column. Grouped summarise with one scalar per group gives one row per group. Source: materials.md L2.

3. Translate “among”

“Among A, what fraction are B?” counts A in the denominator and both A and B in the numerator. Source: materials.md L3.
