1. **Compare denominators.** The morning class has 18 students, including 9 bus commuters. The evening class has 42 students, including 14 bus commuters. Each student appears once, with no missing data. What proportion of the morning class commute by bus? What proportion of all students are morning-class bus commuters? Explain why the denominators differ while the numerators stay the same.

2. **Trace row counts.** A 29-row table has four station groups containing 7, 6, 9, and 7 rows. First group by station, then apply grouped mutate to add a column, then apply grouped summarise to its result, returning one scalar summary per station. All groups remain present, with no filtering. How many rows are present after each step? Explain each count.

3. **Translate conditional language.** Use this complete table, with one row per student:

   | | Robotics club | Not in robotics club |
   |---|---:|---:|
   | Scholarship | 12 | 6 |
   | No scholarship | 18 | 24 |

   Among scholarship students, what fraction belong to robotics? State the numerator, denominator, and fraction. If someone instead divides by the total number of robotics members, what different question does that answer? State that numerator and denominator too.
