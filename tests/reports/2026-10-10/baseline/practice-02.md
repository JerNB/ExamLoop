# STA-DEMO — grouped-proportion question with a small hint

## Q1 — Combine categories within a group

A museum records each visitor exactly once, with no missing data. In Room A, 6 adults and 4 children use an audio guide, and 10 other visitors do not use one. Room B has 30 visitors, of whom 9 use an audio guide. These categories are disjoint and cover all visitors in each room.

What proportion of Room A's visitors use an audio guide? Explain the denominator.

**Small hint:** Before calculating, finish this sentence: “The population I am taking a proportion of is ___.” Then count everyone in that population.
